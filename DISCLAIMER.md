## Research Purpose

Veil explores how modern browsers interact with web servers and creates
synthetic traffic indistinguishable from real browser behavior. The
technology is based on statistical models of Chrome behavior and allows
generating HTTP/2 sessions identical to real browsers at the packet level.

Applications:
- Testing network equipment and traffic analysis systems
- Researching HTTP/2 and TLS 1.3 protocol behavior
- Educational purposes: studying network protocols
- Load testing web servers with realistic traffic patterns
